Stroop test

You will see colour words, one at a time. Each word is painted in a colour of ink. Tap the button for the colour of the ink, not the word. Go as fast as you can.

For example, if you see BLUE, the ink is red, so you tap Red.

This test keeps nothing about you. Your result shows on this screen only. If red and green look alike to you, this test may not work well for you.
